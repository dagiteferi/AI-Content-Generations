# Story brief

Used by `heritage draft` to produce `storyboard.json`.

- **Title:** የመጨረሻው ምዕራፍ (The Final Chapter)
- **Whose story:** two sayings my Ethiopian father told me the night before my Grade 12 national exam
- **Narration language:** Amharic (am), one calm narrator voice, with English subtitles
- **Audience:** Ethiopian Grade 12 students and final-year university students facing the exit exam, and their families. They watch on a phone, vertical, often with the sound off at first.
- **Feeling at the end:** a quiet seriousness about preparing now, then hope and pride. The viewer should think: "my final chapter has started; tonight I open the book."
- **Real people shown or voiced:** none. The student and the father are fictional characters inspired by my family. The narrator is an AI voice, not my father's voice.

## The story in 3-5 sentences
Late at night, a father stands in the doorway of his child's small room and tells them: "ፈተና ቀልድ አያውቅም፤ ውጤትም ይቅርታ የለውም" (an exam knows no jokes, and the result knows no forgiveness). Months earlier the student laughed with friends and left the books closed, but as the exam comes close the father's words come back, and the student starts to study every night by lamp light while the pencil grows shorter. On exam morning the student walks into the hall calm and ready, and writes. On results day the student runs home and the father, smiling, says the second saying: "የመጨረሻው ምዕራፍ ቀልድ አይወድም" (the final chapter does not like jokes). The narrator then turns to the viewer: whether it is the national exam or the exit exam, your final chapter has already started.

## Shot plan (8-9 shots, about 70 seconds)
1. Night, wide: a small Ethiopian house in a quiet neighbourhood, one warm window lit.
2. Medium: the father in the doorway, the student at a small wooden study table. First saying.
3. Medium (memory, slightly warmer and softer): the student laughing with friends outside, a closed textbook on the bench.
4. Close-up: the closed textbook and a long new pencil on the table.
5. Medium: the student opens the book under the lamp and studies, serious and focused.
6. Close-up: the same pencil, now short and worn, next to filled notebook pages. Time has passed.
7. Wide, morning: the student walks toward the exam hall with other students, soft morning light.
8. Medium: results day, the student embraces the father at the door of the house. Second saying.
9. Close-up: the lamp on the empty study table, the book open. Narrator speaks to the viewer.

## Details worth showing (places, objects, sounds, phrases)
- a small wooden study table and an oil or desk lamp at night
- the pencil: long and new at the start, short and worn later (shows time passing without any text)
- a father in a simple gabi or jacket, the student about 18 in a plain shirt
- the exam hall seen from outside in the morning, other students in the distance
- both sayings spoken in Amharic: the first near the start, the second near the end

## Limits for the free models
- Only two characters, described the same way in every shot; the student is in almost every shot.
- No readable text anywhere in the images: no calendars, signs, book titles or exam papers with words.
- No crowds in close shots; other students only far away and out of focus.
- One slow, gentle movement per shot (a slow push-in, a head turn, a page turning). No running in close shots.
- Narration under 130 Amharic words in total, short sentences, so the voice fits the shots.

cinematic realistic digital painting, warm amber lamp light at night, soft golden morning light, muted earth tones, Ethiopian home and school, gentle film grain, natural faces and hands
